Hello! I've been having a ton of fun vibing up self-contained HTML pages to explore random interests and I finally assembled a hosting setup. You can find them all at [https://curious.strangeleaflet.com](https://curious.strangeleaflet.com) and they are literally straight up from the public folder of [curious-strangeleaflet](https://github.com/sdball/strangeleaflet-curious). Very intentionally there's no cohesive theming, no build step, no script at all required. There _is_ a script to serve them all up locally but that's just for convenience the pages work equally well when opened directly as HTML.

You can find a page on how [bitfield encoding](https://curious.strangeleaflet.com/bitfield-explorer/) works which is a much more feature-filled version of the [bitfield explorer](https://strangeleaflet.fly.dev/widgets/bitfield-explorer) I have on my toy Elixir site.

Or maybe mess around with [complex numbers](https://curious.strangeleaflet.com/complex-numbers/)?

Or check out how [morse code](https://curious.strangeleaflet.com/morse-code/) operators were basically doing pro-gamer speed things in the 1800s?

Check out the "text adventure interface" on the index page where you can jump back to like 1983 when I, as a little kid, was completely entranced by ZORK and Enchanter and the like. It's all themed like I remember the INFOCOM games running on our Atari 800XL.

I also finally decided to work up a proper theme for this blog. I hope you enjoy!
